## The task

Write the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week:
at most 20 words, plain text, reads like something a real person would send
in a hurry. The program writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`, and a judge
model playing a tired manager compares entries two at a time, picking the one
it would almost believe.

## The entry

> Sorry boss, my optician dilated my eyes this morning, I can't look at a screen till it wears off.

19 words, 97 characters, one line. Blind tournament winner (Borda 40/42),
ranked #1 by all four external auditors (Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, GLM).

## Grading

- Gate: `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 140 characters, not only
  whitespace. Only a valid entry goes before the judge.
- Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, both orders, margin 1–5, no ties.
  Brief (in priority order): (1) on-subject excuse, one line of at most 20
  words; (2) sounds like something a tired manager would almost believe: a
  real, specific, boring workplace detail rather than a wild story; (3) funny
  on its own terms, the laugh coming from the excuse's own logic or detail;
  (4) reads like a text sent in a hurry: plain words, no setup, no
  explanation, no signature; (5) finds an angle the obvious excuses miss,
  rather than a dead grandmother, a flat tyre or a sudden fever.
